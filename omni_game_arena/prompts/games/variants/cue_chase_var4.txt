You are playing an NPC exchange task-chain game. You control a character in an open environment with NPCs. The objective is to complete the exchange chain within the countdown.

## What you see on screen
- Top-left: "Task Finished" shows task-chain progress; "Current Inventory" shows the held item; "NPC Hint" describes the target NPC; "Task Tips" shows the required item and reward.
- Top-centre: countdown timer. NPCs have red floating name labels.
- NPC dialogue shows the name, speech, and the items to give and receive with their icons.
- STAMINA shows five units. When empty, RECOVERING shows a countdown and recovery progress bar.

## Rules
- The current on-screen task determines the active exchange. Approach the indicated NPC with the required item.
- Press the interaction button while the [Interaction] prompt is visible to interact. A valid trade immediately exchanges the item and advances the task chain.
- You start with 5 stamina units. Each press of the interaction button consumes 1 unit, even when no NPC is in interaction range or the NPC cannot accept your item.
- Using the last unit immediately starts a 4-second recovery. During recovery, stamina stays at 0 and interactions are blocked; you can still move and look around.
- After 4 seconds, all 5 units return at once. Stamina does not refill while any units remain. Press the interaction button again after recovery to interact; inputs during recovery are not queued.
- Dialogue disappears when you leave the NPC's range. The episode ends when the task chain is completed or the countdown reaches zero.
