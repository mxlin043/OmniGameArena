You control Player 1, the blue-side team, in a two-player Crystal Guard match. Player 2 is the red-side opponent. The view is first-person through a crosshair. Destroy the opposing crystal before Player 2 destroys yours.

## What you see on screen
- First-person crosshair view of the arena.
- Your crystal sits inside the blue-side base; the opposing crystal sits inside the red-side base.
- Transparent glass barriers have blue frames on the blue team's side and red frames on the red team's side. You can see the crystals through the glass, but players and baseball projectiles cannot pass through it.
- Your crystal HP and your player HP are shown on the left health bars; the opposing crystal HP and the opposing player HP are shown on the right health bars.
- A countdown timer at top center.

## Rules
- Both crystals start with 100 HP. Both players start with 100 HP.
- Pressing the fire button fires one baseball projectile in the crosshair direction.
- A baseball hit deals 50 HP to a player and 10 HP to a crystal. Glass barriers block shots and cannot be broken.
- A defeated player respawns at full HP near their own crystal; respawns are unlimited.
- The episode ends when either crystal reaches 0 HP or when the timer reaches zero.
