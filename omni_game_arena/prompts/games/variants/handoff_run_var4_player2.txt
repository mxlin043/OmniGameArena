You control Player 2, the red character in a top-down 3D cooperative arena. Player 1 is the blue partner. Maximize the combined Player 1 + Player 2 score before the timer runs out.

## What you see on screen
- Labeled food items, transfer points, and stations are visible in the arena.
- Top-left: Player 1 score. Top-right: Player 2 score.
- Bottom: Game Time countdown and Current orders list (each order shows the required food item and its point value).
- An occupied transfer point shows the item placed there.
- A green label directly above your character shows the item you are carrying. After upgrading it becomes "High-grade <item>". No green label means empty-handed.
- Distinguish your red character from the blue partner; a green label above the partner shows the partner's carried item, not yours.
- Interaction prompts ("PickUp", "Interact", "Upgrade", "Submit", "Discard") appear on screen when you stand close enough to the relevant item, transfer point, or station.
- During workbench processing, "Processing..." and a yellow progress bar appear above the workbench. The bar fills over 4 seconds, stays visible when you move away, and disappears when processing finishes.
- The separate output plate is labeled "Output Plate" while empty and shows "Empty" when approached. When ready, it displays the finished food name and offers "Pickup". The workbench offers "Plate occupied" until the output is taken.

## Rules
- Food supplies are in Player 1's left area. The WorkBench, output plate and Order Counter are in Player 2's right area.
- Both players act in the same cooperative arena and work from the same active order list.
- Four transfer points connect the players' areas; each holds at most one item at a time.
- You can carry at most one item at a time.
- An interaction works only when its matching prompt is visible:
  - PickUp at a floor item or occupied transfer point: hold that item if empty-handed.
  - Interact at an empty transfer point: place the held item there if carrying one.
  - Submit at the Order Counter: if the held item matches an active order, score the order's points and remove it from the list.
  - Upgrade at the WorkBench: place a held normal food item into the workbench. Your hand becomes empty and processing takes 4 seconds of game time.
  - Pickup at the output plate: take the finished High-grade food item if empty-handed. It is golden yellow, and your green carried-item label becomes "High-grade <item>".
  - Discard at the Bin: drop the held item, becoming empty-handed.
- While processing, you may leave the WorkBench and perform other tasks; the partner may continue preparing and transferring food.
- Processing accepts only one normal food item at a time. "Processing..." or "Plate occupied" blocks another upgrade. Empty hands and already High-grade food cannot start processing.
- Completed food stays on the output plate until taken. Full hands cannot pick it up; an occupied output plate prevents the next processing cycle.
- High-grade orders accept only High-grade items; submitting a normal item for a High-grade order does not score.
- The episode ends when Game Time reaches zero.
