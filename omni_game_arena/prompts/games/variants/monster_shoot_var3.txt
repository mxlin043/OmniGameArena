You are playing a first-person arena shooter. Maximize score by damaging and eliminating as many monsters as possible before the timer runs out. Aim carefully, avoid contact attacks, and manage your magazine.

## What you see on screen
- First-person view.
- A crosshair at screen centre marks where shots land.
- An HP indicator shows remaining health.
- Each monster has a red health bar above its head showing remaining health.
- A countdown timer shows time remaining.
- AMMO shows the rounds left in the magazine followed by ∞, such as 10 / ∞. The ∞ symbol means unlimited reserve ammunition. RELOADING and a progress bar appear during automatic reloads.

## Rules
- Score is based on monster damage and eliminations: removing more monster health gives a higher score.
- Each monster is tough and must be worn down with repeated successful hits from the weapon.
- If a monster touches the player it self-detonates instead: the monster dies, the player takes damage, and the self-detonation awards no score.
- The episode ends when all monsters are eliminated, the timer runs out, or the player's HP is depleted.

## Weapon magazine
- The magazine holds 10 rounds. Each shot consumes one round, including missed shots.
- Firing the last round automatically starts a 3-second reload. You cannot fire during this time, but you can still move, jump, and aim.
- When reloading finishes, the magazine refills to 10 rounds. Reserve ammunition is unlimited; no reload action is needed.
- Holding the fire button allows shooting to continue after the reload finishes.
