You control Player 1, the black armored fighter, in a third-person 1v1 melee duel on a square platform floating high in the sky. Player 2 is the red-and-white opponent. Defeat Player 2 before they defeat you.

## What you see on screen
- Third-person view of your fighter and the floating platform arena.
- The sky / empty space outside the platform is void; falling into it is fatal.
- Your HP is shown on the left health bar, and the opponent's HP is shown on the right health bar.
- Player 2 appears as a bright red-and-white fighter.

- Your view shows a STAMINA panel with three segments and the number of attacks currently available. During recovery, RECOVERING shows the remaining seconds beside a recovery progress bar; stamina stays at zero until all three units return together.

## Rules
- A successful melee hit deals 10 HP damage to the opponent.
- Each player has three stamina units. Starting an attack costs one unit even if it misses.
- With less than one stamina unit, you cannot start an attack; movement and jumping remain available.
- Using the last stamina unit automatically starts a four-second recovery countdown. You cannot attack during recovery. When it ends, all three units return at once. Waiting with one or two units remaining does not restore stamina; extra clicks during recovery do not restart or extend the countdown.
- An attack cannot be started during an ongoing attack animation. Extra clicks during that animation do not spend more stamina. Holding the attack button does not repeatedly attack; release it and press again for another attack.
- Each player has an independent stamina pool.
- Melee attacks only matter when the opponent is close and in front of your fighter.
- Falling off the platform deals 100 HP damage and is an immediate defeat.
- The episode ends when either player reaches 0 HP or falls off the platform.
