You control Player 2, the red character in a top-down 3D cooperative arena. Player 1 is the blue partner. Maximize the combined Player 1 + Player 2 score before the timer runs out.

## What you see on screen
- Labeled part objects and stations are visible in the arena.
- Top-left: Player 1 score. Top-right: Player 2 score.
- Bottom: Game Time countdown and Current orders list (each order shows the required part type and its point value).
- A green label directly above your character shows the part you are carrying. After upgrading it becomes "High-grade <part>". No green label means empty-handed.
- Distinguish your red character from the blue partner; a green label above the partner shows the partner's carried part, not yours.
- Interaction prompts appear when you stand close enough: "PickUp" for floor parts, "Pickup" for finished parts on the output plate, "Upgrade" at the WorkBench, "Submit" at the Order Counter, and "Discard" at the Bin.
- Workbench status: "Processing..." means a part is being processed; "Plate occupied" means its finished part must be collected before another part can be processed. The workbench shows "Upgrade" again when available.
- An empty output plate is labeled "Output Plate" and shows "Empty" when approached. A ready plate displays the finished part's name and shows "Pickup" when approached. "Processing...", "Plate occupied", and "Empty" indicate that the corresponding upgrade or pickup action is unavailable.
- During processing, a yellow progress bar above the WorkBench fills from left to right and remains visible when you move away. It disappears when processing finishes; the finished part's name then appears above the output plate.

## Rules
- Both players act in the same shared arena and work from the same active order list.
- You can carry at most one part at a time.
- An interaction works only when its matching prompt is visible:
  - PickUp at a floor part: hold that part.
  - Submit at the Order Counter: if the held part matches an active order, score the order's points and remove it from the list.
  - Upgrade at the WorkBench: place a normal part into the workbench. Your hands become empty and a progress bar above the workbench fills over 4 seconds. You may move away and do other tasks while it processes.
  - When processing finishes, the High-grade part appears on the output plate beside the workbench. Its name is displayed above the plate. Approach the plate empty-handed and use Pickup to take it. Either player may collect it.
  - Each workbench processes one part at a time. It cannot accept another part during processing or while a finished part remains on its output plate. Take the finished part to free the workbench.
  - An empty hand or an already High-grade part cannot start processing. A player carrying another part cannot pick up from the output plate.
  - Discard at the Bin: drop the held part, becoming empty-handed.
- High-grade orders accept only High-grade parts; submitting a normal part for a High-grade order does not score.
- The episode ends when Game Time reaches zero.
